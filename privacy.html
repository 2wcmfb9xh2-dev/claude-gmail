Privacy Policy – Claude Gmail

Claude Gmail is a private application used solely by its owner (Tadeusz Lakota) to access his own Gmail account through the AI assistant Claude running on his own computer.

Data accessed

The application reads, searches and creates drafts in the owner's own Gmail account. It does not send, delete or modify emails.

Storage and sharing

Authorization tokens are stored only locally on the owner's computer. No data is stored on any server operated by this application, and no data is sold or shared with third parties. Email content is processed only on the owner's request by the AI assistant he uses.

Use of Google user data

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
